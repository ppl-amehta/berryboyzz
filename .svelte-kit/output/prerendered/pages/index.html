Proprietary Agronomic Framework

The 6-Pillar Biological Operating System

Commercial berry cultivation in Maharashtra fails when treated like open-field farming. Our integrated Biological OS governs every parameter from tissue culture multiplication to cold-dock supermarket acceptance.

01 GENOMICS-TC

High-Genetics Tissue Culture & Imported Clones

Virus-indexed, certified disease-free micropropagated plants from world-class European and North American nurseries.

"Meristem-tip micropropagation eliminates systemic viral loads. Primocane varieties guarantee fruiting on current-season first-year wood without mandatory chilling cycles."
Key Deliverables:
  • Genetic authenticity certificate for every clonal batch
  • Complete lab screening report against raspberry bushy dwarf virus & latent ringspot
  • Hardened, plug-established saplings with active mycorrhizal root colonization
Audit Frequency: At batch dispatch & 30 days post-transplant
02 RHIZO-BACTO

Fertilizer Input & Bacto-Rich Rhizosphere Development

Proprietary slurry development and fermented microbial consortia to stimulate hyper-vigorous nutrient uptake.

"Liquid formulations of Bacillus subtilis, Trichoderma harzianum, and Azospirillum solubilize bound phosphorus and chelate micronutrients while stimulating plant growth regulators (PGRs)."
Key Deliverables:
  • Customized monthly fertigation schedule matched to plant phenology
  • Concentrated Bacto-Liquid microbial inoculants supplied per acre
  • Organic PGR boosters formulated for drupelet swelling & Brix maximization
Audit Frequency: Bi-weekly substrate leachate testing
03 OPS-CORE

Operating System & Dedicated Agronomy Team

Standard operating protocols (SOPs) governing every hour of farm management from sunrise irrigation to evening canopy management.

"Systematic task queues eliminate grower guesswork. Daily logs track fertigation volume, canopy leaf-area index, pest scouting thresholds, and flower-to-fruit transformation rates."
Key Deliverables:
  • Digitized Farm OS task management dashboard
  • Dedicated on-site farm supervisor training and certification
  • Direct agronomy hotline with 2-hour response SLA for operational emergencies
Audit Frequency: Continuous digital logging
04 AGRO-AUDIT

Agronomist Field Visits & Diagnostic Schedules

Senior horticultural agronomists conduct scheduled physical site audits with precision diagnostic equipment.

"Handheld chlorophyll meters (SPAD), digital refractometers (Brix), and sap-analysis tools detect nutrient deficiencies 10 days before visual leaf symptoms emerge."
Key Deliverables:
  • Comprehensive physical audit report with actionable photographic action items
  • Immediate chemical & biological intervention adjustments
  • Seasonal pruning and canopy architecture optimization guidance
Audit Frequency: Bi-monthly scheduled physical audits
05 COLD-CHAIN

Cold-Chain Logistics & Controlled Atmosphere Transit

Unbroken refrigerated transit from farm gate to supermarket shelf, arresting respiration within 45 minutes of harvest.

"Forced-air rapid pre-cooling pulls core berry pulp temperature down to 2°C–4°C, lowering ethylene synthesis and extending fresh punnet shelf life from 3 days to 14 days."
Key Deliverables:
  • GPS-tracked temperature-logged reefer vans servicing Pune, Mumbai, and Goa daily
  • Export-grade vented 125g punnets with food-grade anti-microbial absorbent pads
  • Guaranteed cold-dock acceptance protocols with tier-1 luxury retailers
Audit Frequency: Per-shipment temperature data-logger telemetry
06 OFF-TAKE

Guaranteed B2B Off-Take Partnerships & Market Linkages

Contractual off-take agreements with luxury hotel chains, modern trade grocers, and pastry institutions eliminating market price volatility.
