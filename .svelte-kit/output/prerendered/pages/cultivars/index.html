Imported Primocane cultivars engineered for high-density CEA polyhouses in Pune and Satara foothills. Yields firm, velvety aggregate drupelets with explosive sweet-tart aromatic profiles. Annual harvest twice every year.

Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus

Thornless, ultra-vigorous canes adapted to Satara plateau micro-climates. Produces massive, glossy aggregate drupelets exceeding 8 grams each with intense antioxidant density. Yields 1.6 kg per harvest twice a year.

Sugar Index: 14.5° Brix
Substrate: Volcanic cinder + Coir husk chips (60:40) with active mycorrhizal inoculation
₹350 / punnet Dossier

Mulberry (Shahtoot)

Morus macroura / Morus nigra

Long exotic finger-length mulberries reaching up to 10cm. Extremely high sugar accumulation (up to 21° Brix) with zero tannin bitterness. Premium delicacy in high-end culinary gastronomy. Packaged at ₹80 for 100g / 200g punnets.

Sugar Index: 19.5° Brix
Substrate: Deep sandy-loam fortified with composted bagasse and trichoderma
₹80 (100g/200g) Dossier
Bi-Annual Flush 1.6 kg/yr

Strawberry

Fragaria × ananassa

Elevated gutter hydroponic strawberries cultivating dense, uniform conical berries with deep red core saturation, superior firmness, and elevated Brix levels outperforming standard field varieties. High-density commercial setup.

Sugar Index: 12.5° Brix
Substrate: Sterilized Dutch Coco-Coir slabs with dual micro-drip emitters per plant
₹180 / punnet Dossier
Mature Yield: 36 Mos 2.5 kg/yr

Blueberry

Vaccinium corymbosum

The highest margin berry crop globally. Cultivated in 35-liter aerated grow bags with acidic coir substrate and RO fertigation. Produces firm, powder-bloomed berries with crunchy skin. Reaches full mature yield of 2.5 kg per plant after 36 months.

Sugar Index: 15.2° Brix
Substrate: Specialized Acidified Pine bark + coarse peat (pH strictly 4.5–5.0)
₹2,500 / kg mature Dossier
