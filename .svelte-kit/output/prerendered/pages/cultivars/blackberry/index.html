Blackberry

Rubus fruticosus

Thornless, ultra-vigorous canes adapted to Satara plateau micro-climates. Produces massive, glossy aggregate drupelets exceeding 8 grams each with intense antioxidant density. Yields 1.6 kg per harvest twice a year.

Micro-Climate & Rhizosphere

Temperature Target: 18°C night / 30°C day
Chilling Hours: 150 - 200 hours low-chill winter requirement
Substrate pH Range: 6.0 – 6.4 Target pH
Relative Humidity (RH): 55% – 65% RH
Fertigation EC: 1.6 – 2.0 mS/cm Target EC

Daily Field Protocol

  • Caning & tipping at 1.4m height to force vigorous secondary fruiting laterals
  • Bacto-slurry potassium-rich organic fertigation during drupelet cell division stage
  • UV-blocking 30% thermal shading during peak midday sun in March-May
  • Immediate field pre-cooling to 4°C within 45 minutes of harvest to preserve cell turgidity
